# Split

One cart. Multiple people. Zero awkward math.

Split makes it easy to decide who pays for each item in a group shopping cart. The MVP starts with Alex, Jordan, and Sam shopping for Apartment Essentials. Visitors can also create an empty cart.

The demo follows a single journey from opening the cart through collaborating, splitting costs, reviewing checkout, and viewing a shareable receipt. Checkout is simulated. Authentication, payments, and merchant integrations are outside this release.
